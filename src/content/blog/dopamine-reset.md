## The Modern Attention Crisis

Your brain is under constant assault. Between infinite scroll, algorithmic notifications, and hyper-palatable digital content, your baseline dopamine levels are likely depleted. This isn't a failure of willpower; it's an engineering mismatch between your evolutionary neurobiology and modern technology.

When your dopamine baseline drops, you experience:
* Chronic procrastination on high-effort tasks
* A constant underlying sense of restlessness
* Brain fog and difficulty sustaining attention

## The 48-Hour Reset Protocol

To restore your neurochemical balance, you don't need to move to a cabin in the woods forever. A targeted **48-hour dopamine fast** can reset your baseline sensitivity.

### Step 1: The Elimination Phase (Hours 0-24)
Remove all high-dopamine triggers. This means absolute zero:
* Social media and short-form video
* Video games
* Refined sugar and junk food
* Streaming platforms (Netflix, YouTube, etc.)

Expect severe psychological friction during this phase. Your brain will crave its usual hits.

### Step 2: The Boredom Tolerance Phase (Hours 24-48)
Replace the void with low-dopamine, high-effort activities.
* **Read physical books:** This trains sustained, linear attention.
* **Write:** Journaling forces your brain to process thoughts rather than passively consuming them.
* **Exercise:** Zone 2 cardio (like jogging or brisk walking) naturally elevates dopamine and serotonin without subsequent crashes.

After 48 hours, mundane tasks will feel surprisingly engaging. The goal isn't to abstain from technology forever, but to regain conscious control over your attention allocation.

You are the CEO of your neurochemistry. Manage it deliberately.
